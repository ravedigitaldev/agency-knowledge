# Second-brain curation policy
Only add durable, reusable, verified operational knowledge from approved project outcomes. Require:
- Title, date, responsible reviewer and source links (project, PR, ADR or measured retrospective).
- Confirmed finding and evidence (not speculative generated text).
- Applicable contexts and explicit exceptions.
- Confidence and known expiry/review trigger.
- An exact filename in `principles/`, `decisions/`, `standards/`, `patterns/` or `lessons/`.

Knowledge is **retrieved per project/task**; workers never preload the full repo. Remove/redact client-identifying details. History archive is not this repository.
